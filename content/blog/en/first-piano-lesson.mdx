A first lesson can feel like a big step, especially for a young child. Here is exactly what happens in the 30-minute trial, so there are no surprises.

## Meet and play

We start by getting comfortable. I ask about favorite songs, and the student explores the piano: high and low, loud and soft, the black keys and the white ones.

## A few small games

Clapping a rhythm back to me, finding all the C's on the keyboard, singing a short phrase. These games show me how a student hears, moves and focuses — no preparation needed.

## A first tiny piece

Almost every student leaves the first lesson able to play something short with one hand. That moment of "I played it!" matters more than anything else on day one.

## A plan for the parents

In the last few minutes I share what I noticed and suggest the right lesson length and a plan for the first months. You can ask anything — about practice at home, the instrument or the schedule.

<Tip>
Nothing to bring, nothing to prepare. Comfortable clothes and short nails are all a child needs.
</Tip>
