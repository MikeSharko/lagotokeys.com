Parents often ask me for one magic number. There isn't one, but for most children private lessons start to work well somewhere between five and seven. Age matters less than a few simple signs of readiness.

## Signs your child is ready

- They can sit and focus on one activity for about fifteen minutes.
- They know their numbers and the first letters of the alphabet — we use both to name fingers and notes.
- They can move fingers one at a time, for example showing you just the ring finger.
- Most important: they ask about the piano and enjoy pressing the keys.

## If your child is a little too young

That's completely fine. Sing together, clap simple rhythms, listen to music and let them explore the piano without rules. A group music class for little ones is a wonderful first step. When the signs above appear, a 30-minute weekly lesson is the right start.

<Tip>
The trial lesson is the best way to find out. In half an hour I can usually tell whether a child is ready now or would enjoy it more in a few months — and I'll tell you honestly.
</Tip>

## And older beginners?

There is no "too late." Children who start at nine or ten often move faster, because reading and concentration are already in place.
