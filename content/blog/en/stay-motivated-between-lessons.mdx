Every student has weeks when the piano feels like a chore. That's normal. These three simple habits help students keep going until the next breakthrough.

## 1. Small, clear goals

"Practice the piece" is too vague. "Play bars 5 to 8 without stopping" is a goal you can finish today. Each week I write two or three of these goals in the student's notebook.

## 2. A practice chart

A sticker or a checkmark for every day at the piano — it sounds simple, and it works for adults too. A full week earns a small reward agreed in advance at home.

## 3. An audience at home

Once a week, hold a mini concert: one or two pieces for the family after dinner. Grandparents on a video call count too. Playing for someone gives practice a real purpose.

<Tip>
If motivation drops for more than a couple of weeks, tell me. Often a new piece or a different style is all it takes to bring the spark back.
</Tip>

Our student concerts twice a year work the same way, just on a bigger stage: a date on the calendar and a piece to be proud of.
